## Purpose

Prepare coronal cerebellar sections of chicken embryo brain for H&E staining and immunohistochemistry (protocol 21) and for quantitative layer measurements (protocol 23).

## Before you start

Formalin is a hazardous fixative. Work in a fume hood. Record the egg, the embryo number, the body mass and the treatment group with a coded identifier, so that later scoring can be blinded (protocol 23).

## Procedure

1. **Expose and sample.** Expose embryos at E16 and harvest on E17 (protocol 10). Decapitate, weigh the embryo and note the body mass. The studies excluded the embryos with the highest and the lowest body mass (protocol 10).
2. **Open the skull** along the cranial sutures.
3. **Fix.** Fix whole heads in 10% buffered formalin (Chemi-Teknik AS, Oslo) for 24 hours at +4 °C. The 2022 paper adds constant agitation.
4. **Remove the brain** from the skull and divide it in the coronal plane, as described in Austdal et al. 2016.
5. **Process.** Process the tissue in a vacuum infiltration tissue processor (Tissue-Tek VIP, Sakura Finetek USA), orient it and embed it in paraffin.
6. **Section.** Cut coronal sections of **3 µm** from the paraffin blocks and mount them on SuperFrost Plus slides.
7. **Stain.** Stain with hematoxylin and eosin (H&E) by standard protocols. The 2022 paper uses H&E as the control staining to confirm the cytomorphological organisation, to separate the cerebellar layers and to identify granule neurons by their small, round, hyperchromatic nuclei and sparse cytoplasm.
8. **Scan** the stained slides (protocol 23).

## Quality checks

- Cerebellar layers must be clearly separable on H&E (external granular layer, molecular layer, internal granular layer and white matter).
- Use sections with optimal orientation for the measurements (protocol 23).

## Not stated in the papers

- the dehydration and clearing steps and times in the tissue processor
- the paraffin infiltration schedule and temperatures
- the standard H&E staining times
- the coronal plane position in the Austdal 2016 protocol

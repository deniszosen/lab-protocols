## Purpose

Fix spinal cord organoids, section them on a cryostat and label sections for transcription factors that identify motor neurons and spinal interneurons.

## Before you start

- Paraformaldehyde (PFA) is toxic. Prepare and use it in a fume hood.
- Record the organoid batch, line and day in culture for every sample.

## Materials

| Item | Details as stated |
|---|---|
| Fixative | 4% PFA in PBS, pH 7.4 |
| Cryoprotection | 30% sucrose in PBS |
| Embedding | OCT compound |
| Block | TBS with 10% goat serum and 0.2% Triton X-100 |
| Wash buffer | TBS with 1% goat serum |
| Antibodies | Listed in Table S4 of the paper. The paper reports labelling for ISLET1 and HB9 (motor neurons) and LHX1/5 and PAX2 (immature spinal interneurons). |
| Counterstain | DAPI |
| Mounting | 50% PBS/glycerol |
| Microscope | Zeiss LSM700 confocal at the Norwegian Core Facility for Human Pluripotent Stem Cells. Images processed in Zen Lite. |

## Procedure

1. Harvest organoids at DIV 24 and DIV 32 (protocol 07).
2. Fix in 4% PFA in PBS (pH 7.4) overnight at 4 °C.
3. Transfer to 30% sucrose in PBS for 24 hours.
4. Embed in OCT compound and store at −80 °C.
5. Cut sections of **10 µm** on a cryostat.
6. Wash the sections with PBS.
7. Block in TBS with 10% goat serum and 0.2% Triton X-100 for 2 hours at room temperature.
8. Apply the primary antibodies and incubate overnight at 4 °C.
9. Rinse with TBS containing 1% goat serum.
10. Incubate with the secondary antibodies for 2 hours at room temperature.
11. Counterstain the nuclei with DAPI and mount the coverslips in 50% PBS/glycerol.
12. Image on the confocal microscope and process the images.

## Sample size as reported

For the quantitative analyses, 4 to 5 organoids at DIV 24 and 3 to 4 organoids at DIV 32 were assessed, unless the paper states otherwise.

## Not stated in the paper

- the section thickness variation between organoids and the number of sections per organoid
- antigen retrieval, if any
- the antibody dilutions (in Table S4)
- the secondary antibody details

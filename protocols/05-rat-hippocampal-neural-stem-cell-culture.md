## Purpose

Isolate neural stem cells from the hippocampus of rats at postnatal day 14 (P14), differentiate them with retinoic acid, and measure proliferation with BrdU. The original study compared Krushinskii-Molodkina (KM) rats, which are genetically prone to audiogenic seizures, with Wistar controls.

## Before you start

- Rat work needs an approved animal project. The original work was approved by the ethics committee of the Sechenov Institute and followed the NIH Guide for the care and use of laboratory animals. A study in Norway needs its own approval. See chapter 11 of the [lab handbook](https://github.com/deniszosen/lab-handbook).
- Sterile technique throughout. The paper states that all manipulations were done in a sterile laminar flow hood.

## Materials

| Item | Details as stated |
|---|---|
| Animals | KM and Wistar rats at P14. The study used 8 animals, 4 per group. P14 was chosen because the subgranular zone of the dentate gyrus is fully formed by then. |
| Dissection medium | Cold (4 °C) Hanks medium (BioloT) |
| Digestion | Trypsin/EDTA solution (concentration not stated) |
| Culture medium | DMEM and F12 1:1 (Gibco; Sigma) with 10% horse serum (Gibco), 8% fetal calf serum (Sigma), B27 supplement (Gibco) and penicillin-streptomycin |
| Coating | Collagen type IV (Roche) |
| Differentiation | Retinoic acid (Sigma), added on days 5 and 7 |
| Proliferation label | Bromodeoxyuridine (BrdU), added on days 1 to 3 for one group and days 7 to 9 for the second group |

## Procedure

1. Decapitate the rat and remove the hippocampus into cold Hanks medium.
2. Mince the tissue to a uniform material with a scalpel.
3. Digest in trypsin/EDTA for 30 minutes at 37 °C.
4. Pellet the cells at 1000 rpm for 10 minutes.
5. Resuspend in the culture medium and plate onto collagen IV-coated plates or coverslips.
6. Renew the medium every 2 days (days 3, 5, 7 and 9).
7. Add retinoic acid on days 5 and 7 to stimulate neuronal differentiation.
8. Assess proliferation by adding BrdU on days 1 to 3 (first group) or days 7 to 9 (second group).
9. On day 10, collect the cells for immunocytochemistry (protocol 15, formalin variant) and western blot (protocol 26, cell-lysate variant).

## What the original study measured

Sox2 and nestin (stem cell markers), MAP2, doublecortin, NeuN and VGLUT2 (neuronal markers), GFAP (glial marker) and tyrosine hydroxylase. Cells were counted in ImageJ against DAPI-stained nuclei. Colocalisation of Sox2/nestin, MAP2/GFAP and BrdU with GFAP, TH or NeuN described the functional state.

## Not stated in the paper: set and record these

- trypsin/EDTA concentration, B27 dilution, retinoic acid concentration and BrdU concentration
- seeding density, plate format and coating concentration
- how the cells were detached for counting
- the Methods text says "in vivo on primary cell cultures". This appears to be a translation slip. The cells were cultured in vitro.

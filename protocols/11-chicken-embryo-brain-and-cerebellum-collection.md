## Purpose

Take brains or cerebella from exposed chicken embryos at defined times after dosing, for drug quantification, protein or RNA work, or histology.

## Before you start

Check the approval and welfare conditions in protocol 10. Work quickly and keep tissue cold. Record the time of injection, the time of sampling and the embryonic day for every sample.

## Procedure

### Anaesthesia and euthanasia

1. **Anaesthetise by hypothermia.** Submerge the eggs in crushed ice for 7 minutes before the embryo is taken out. This is done before hatching and decapitation. For the 5-minute exposure group of the 2021 study, the cooling time was shortened and counted as part of the exposure time.
2. **Decapitate.**

### Brain for drug or molecular analysis (2021 and 2025)

3. Open the skull, remove the cranium and detach the whole brain. Remove the meninges with forceps (2021 paper).
4. Take the whole brain or the cerebellum, as the study design needs (2025 paper).
5. Freeze quickly in liquid nitrogen at −196 °C (2021) and store at −80 °C until processing (2025).

### Time points used in the papers

| Paper | Time after the dose |
|---|---|
| 2021 | 5, 15 and 30 minutes, and 1, 2, 4, 6, 12 and 24 hours |
| 2025 | 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 12, 18, 24, 48 and 72 hours. Additional samples were taken on E17. |

### Cerebellum for western blot (2022)

6. Keep cerebella on ice and homogenise them with a pellet pestle (protocol 26).

### Whole head for histology (2022 and 2025)

7. Weigh the embryo after decapitation and note the body mass. The studies excluded the highest and the lowest body masses (protocol 10).
8. Open the skull along the cranial sutures.
9. Fix whole heads in 10% buffered formalin (Chemi-Teknik AS, Oslo) at 4 °C for 24 hours. The 2022 paper adds overnight fixation at +4 °C with constant agitation.
10. Remove the fixed brain from the skull and divide it in the coronal plane, as in the lab's earlier protocol (Austdal et al. 2016, cited in the papers).
11. Continue with protocol 20.

## Not stated in the papers: set and record these

- the tools and the ice-cold buffer used during dissection
- the fixation volume and the agitation speed
- the dissection time per animal and how long samples wait before freezing
- the coronal division plane in the Austdal 2016 protocol, which is cited and not described in these papers

## Purpose

Isolate RNA, including small RNA (miRNA), from snap-frozen embryonic chicken cerebellum and from SH-SY5Y cells, for small RNA sequencing (protocol 31) and qPCR (protocol 32).

## Materials

- RNeasy Plus Mini Kit (Qiagen), for cerebellum and SH-SY5Y cells.
- RNeasy MinElute Cleanup Kit (Qiagen), for additional purification of small RNA (shorter than 200 nucleotides).
- miRNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen 217004), for cerebella harvested on E14.
- Autoclaved electric homogeniser with a plastic pestle. 21-gauge syringe and needle.

## Procedure

1. Inject chicken embryos with 0.9% NaCl, with or without escitalopram or venlafaxine, on E13 and harvest on E14 or E17 (protocol 10). Snap-freeze cerebella (protocol 11).
2. **Validate first.** Run a test isolation on an untreated cerebellum to check the method and the yield and purity.
3. Homogenise the snap-frozen cerebellum with the autoclaved electric homogeniser and plastic pestle, then pass it through a 21-gauge needle on a syringe.
4. Isolate RNA with the RNeasy Plus Mini Kit (cerebellum and SH-SY5Y cells). For E14 cerebella, use the miRNeasy Mini Kit.
5. Purify the small RNA fraction with the RNeasy MinElute Cleanup Kit.
6. Store the RNA at −80 °C.

## Not stated in the paper

- the elution volumes and the quantity of tissue per isolation
- the yield and purity thresholds (RNA concentration and integrity)
- the DNase treatment, beyond the genomic DNA removal built into the kit

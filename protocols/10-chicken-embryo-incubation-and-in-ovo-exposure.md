## Purpose

Incubate fertilised chicken eggs and expose embryos to a drug by a single injection into the allantois. The model was developed to study drug distribution to the developing brain and the effects of drugs on brain development in a 3Rs-friendly system.

## Before you start: approval and welfare

- The papers state that a chicken embryo is defined as an animal from embryonic day 14 (E14) under the Norwegian Animal Welfare Act and the EU Directive 2010/63/EU. Exposure at E13 happens before that point, and exposure at E16 and sampling at E17 happen after it.
- The papers quote the approval FOTS id 13,896 (Norwegian Food Safety Authority). That number belongs to the original study. **A new project needs its own approval.** Check with your institution's animal welfare body, before you start, which embryo stages need approval. See chapters 7 and 11 of the [lab handbook](https://github.com/deniszosen/lab-handbook).
- Apply the 3Rs. The lab uses human cells and tissue where they can answer the question (chapter 7).

## Materials

| Item | Details as stated |
|---|---|
| Eggs | Fertilised broiler eggs, Gallus gallus domesticus, Ross 308 strain (55 to 60 g in the 2021 and 2022 papers, 55 to 65 g in 2025), from Nortura Samvirkekylling, Våler, Norway |
| Incubator | OvaEasy 380 Advance EXII (Brinsea, Weston-super-Mare, UK) at 37.5 °C and 45% relative humidity. Eggs sit in racks that are mechanically tilted to mimic brooding. |
| Light source | Transillumination, to find an injection site and to confirm viability (2025 paper) |
| Needle | 29-gauge (2025 paper) |
| Solvent | Saline (0.9% NaCl) |

## Procedure

1. **Incubate.** Place the eggs in the incubator at 37.5 °C and 45% humidity, with the tilting rack running. The papers did not determine the sex of the embryos. The results are average effects on males and females.
2. **Check viability before each injection.** Use transillumination to confirm the embryo is alive and to choose an injection site that avoids blood vessels (2025 paper).
3. **Prepare the dose.** Dissolve the drug in saline (protocol 13). Inject a volume equal to **1 µL per gram of egg** (a 1:1000 dilution of the intended dose).
4. **Inject into the allantois** (the allantoic sac) with the needle, carefully avoiding blood vessels. Inject on the embryonic day the study design needs: E13, E16, or both, as in the papers.
5. **Controls.** Use saline-injected eggs. The 2022 paper also used non-injected eggs.
6. **Sample** at the time points the design needs (protocol 11).

## Doses used in the papers

| Paper | Compound and dose |
|---|---|
| 2021 | Valproate (VPA) 100 mM (16.6 mg/mL) or lamotrigine (LTG) 5 mM (1.9 mg/mL) in saline, at E13 or E16 |
| 2022 | At E16: VPA 0.17, 1.7, 17, 83 or 166 mg/kg. LTG 0.4, 3.8 or 9.5 mg/kg. For histology: saline, 83 mg/kg VPA, or 3.8 mg/kg LTG. |
| 2025 | At E13 and E16: escitalopram 0.3 mM (0.1 mg/kg) or venlafaxine 1.27 mM (0.35 mg/kg) in 0.9% NaCl |

## Group size and exclusions

- The 2022 paper repeated each experiment three times, with at least three animals per group, so n was at least 9. Measurements from individual embryos count as independent values.
- For histology, the 2022 and 2025 papers weighed each embryo after decapitation and excluded the embryos with the highest and the lowest body mass to limit variability. The 2025 paper used three independent replicates with 5 to 6 eggs per group.
- Write the exclusion rule into your plan before you start, as chapter 7 of the handbook asks.

## Not stated in the papers: set and record these

- egg storage time and conditions before incubation
- how the injection hole is made, sealed and labelled
- the embryonic day numbering convention (which day is E0)
- the criteria for dead or non-viable embryos and how they are recorded
- the needle gauge for the 2021 and 2022 studies

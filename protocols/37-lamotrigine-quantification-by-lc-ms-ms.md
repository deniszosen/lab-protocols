## Purpose

Measure lamotrigine (LTG) in whole-brain homogenates by protein precipitation and LC-MS/MS.

## Before you start

Same conditions as protocol 36: work with an analytical facility, use the animal permit of the original study only for that study, and keep samples on ice throughout.

## Materials

- LTG standards at 0.05, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 3 and 6 µM. Controls at 0.15 and 1.5 µM.
- Internal standard: methaqualone, 5 µM and 10 µM working solutions.
- 5 mM ammonium acetate buffer (pH 3.1) in methanol (50:50). Cold acetonitrile/methanol (85:15). Water.
- Untreated chicken brain homogenate for the standards.
- An LC-MS/MS system (Waters).

## Procedure

1. Homogenise the brain in cold MQ water as in protocol 36 (volume in µL equal to twice the weight in mg).
2. Prepare the standards in untreated brain homogenate in the same way.
3. Precipitate with the ammonium acetate buffer in methanol (50:50).
4. Add 50 µL of 5 µM methaqualone as the internal standard.
5. Add 500 µL of cold acetonitrile/methanol (85:15), mix, and incubate at −20 °C for 10 minutes.
6. Centrifuge for 10 minutes at 4500 rpm and 4 °C.
7. Add 100 µL water, then move 100 µL of the acetonitrile/methanol phase to an autosampler vial.
8. Analyse by LC-MS/MS. Include both control levels in every run.
9. Keep all samples on crushed ice during extraction.

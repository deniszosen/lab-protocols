## Purpose

Keep human induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) in feeder-free conditions and prepare cultures that are fit for organoid differentiation (protocol 07).

## Before you start: consent, approvals and agreements

- The paper used the line HPSI0114i-vabj_3 from the Wellcome Trust Sanger Institute and ECACC, under a materials transfer agreement for academic, non-commercial research. The original derivation and distribution followed the approvals and donor consent arrangements of the HipSci/Sanger resource. No new human samples were collected.
- **A new line, or the same line in a new lab, needs its own agreement and approvals.** Record the donor source, the consent scope, the agreement and the approval before you thaw a vial. See chapters 7 and 11 of the [lab handbook](https://github.com/deniszosen/lab-handbook).
- Authenticate the line by genotype on arrival and at set intervals. Check the karyotype and pluripotency at agreed passage numbers.

## Materials

| Item | Details as stated |
|---|---|
| Cells | Human iPSC line HPSI0114i-vabj_3 (used at about passage 10 for organoid generation) |
| Medium | StemFlex Medium (ThermoFisher A3349401) |
| Coating | Cultrex ECM (Bio-Techne 3434-010-02), 10 µg/cm², on 6-well plates |
| Passaging | 0.5 mM EDTA in PBS |

## Procedure

1. Coat 6-well plates with Cultrex ECM at 10 µg/cm².
2. Culture the cells in StemFlex medium and refresh the medium every other day.
3. When the cells reach 70 to 80% confluence, passage them with 0.5 mM EDTA in PBS at a 1:5 ratio and reseed.
4. Use only cultures with no signs of spontaneous differentiation and high viability for organoid differentiation.
5. When you are ready to differentiate, dissociate the cells with Accutase (protocol 07, step 1).

## Not stated in the paper: set and record these

- the EDTA incubation time and temperature, and how the colonies are collected and counted
- the Cultrex coating incubation conditions
- freezing and thawing conditions and the use of a ROCK inhibitor
- the schedule for mycoplasma tests, karyotype and pluripotency checks
- the passage range allowed for experiments

## Purpose

Detect neurotransmitter system proteins in rat striatum and substantia nigra sections with a biotin-streptavidin-peroxidase DAB method, and quantify the staining by optical density.

## Materials

- Fixed hemisphere: 4% paraformaldehyde in pre-cooled phosphate buffer (protocol 12).
- Primary antibodies and dilutions: GAD67 (1:750, Millipore), GAD65 (1:750, Abcam), VGLUT2 (1:200, Abcam), phospho-Synapsin 1 (1:500, ProSci), phospho-ERK1/2 Thr202/Thr204 (1:250, Cell Signaling), phospho-tyrosine hydroxylase Ser31 (1:1000, Millipore), tyrosine hydroxylase (1:1000, Sigma-Aldrich), and D1 and D2 receptors (1:1000, Millipore).
- Biotinylated secondary antibodies (Vector Labs) and streptavidin-peroxidase complex (Sigma-Aldrich).
- 0.05% diaminobenzidine (DAB) with 0.01% hydrogen peroxide.
- Zeiss Axio Imager A1 microscope. Video-Test software for optical density.

## Procedure

1. After the standard pretreatment, incubate the sections with the primary antibody for 12 hours at room temperature.
2. Wash with PBS.
3. Incubate with the biotinylated secondary antibody, then with the streptavidin-peroxidase complex.
4. Develop the reaction with 0.05% DAB and 0.01% H2O2.
5. Stain **every section for one protein at the same time** in the same run.
6. Include a negative control by omitting the primary antibody. The paper reports no staining in these sections.
7. Image under light microscopy.
8. Measure the optical density of immunopositive staining in neuronal bodies or fibres with the Video-Test software. In some cases the paper counts neurons with intense staining. Express the data in arbitrary units and compare groups as in protocol 12.

## Not stated in the paper

- "the standard pretreatment" (sectioning method, thickness, antigen retrieval, blocking)
- the incubation times for the secondary antibody and the streptavidin complex
- the DAB development time

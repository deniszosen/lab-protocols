## Purpose

Label PAX6 on 3 µm paraffin sections of chicken cerebellum with an automated slide stainer, for scoring in digital images.

## Materials

- Paraffin sections (protocol 20) on SuperFrost Plus slides.
- Primary antibody: anti-PAX6 (Sigma-Aldrich AB2237), dilution 1:12000.
- Roche BenchMark ULTRA automated slide stainer with the OptiView DAB IHC detection kit.
- Companion H&E sections for morphology.

## Procedure

1. Prepare 3 µm coronal cerebellar sections as in protocol 20.
2. Perform the immunohistochemical reaction according to standard protocols, as described in Austdal et al. 2016 and Bjørnstad et al. 2014.
3. Apply the anti-PAX6 antibody at 1:12000. The antigen-antibody reaction runs on the Roche BenchMark ULTRA with the OptiView DAB detection kit.
4. Scan the stained slides (protocol 23) and use the corresponding PAX6 sections for the later analyses.

## Not stated in the paper

- the antigen retrieval conditions and the antibody incubation time
- the stainer programme name and settings
- the negative-control procedure for this antibody

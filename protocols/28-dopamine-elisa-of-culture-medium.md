## Purpose

Measure the dopamine that differentiated PC12 cells release into their medium after MEK/ERK inhibition.

## Materials

- Dopamine ELISA kit (GenWay Biotech, #40-371-25013), used according to the manufacturer's instructions.
- Medium samples collected from NGF-differentiated PC12 cells treated with 10 µM U0126 (Tocris 1144) for 1, 2 or 4 hours in plain DMEM (protocol 03, variant B).
- A plate reader suitable for the kit.

## Procedure

1. Grow PC12 cells on collagen-coated plates to about 50 to 60% confluence. Switch to DMEM with 1% horse serum and 50 ng/mL NGF (Millipore 01-125) and keep them with NGF for 6 days (protocol 03, variant B).
2. On the next day, change the medium to DMEM only and add 10 µM U0126 (Tocris 1144) for 1, 2 or 4 hours (n = 6 per time point).
3. At each time point, collect the medium for the ELISA. Homogenise the cells from the same wells for western blotting (protocol 26).
4. Determine dopamine in the media with the ELISA kit, following the manufacturer's instructions.
5. Compare groups with Student's t-test and report the mean ± SE (p < 0.05).

## Not stated in the paper

- sample handling between collection and the assay, including storage temperature and any stabiliser
- sample dilution
- the plate reader and the standard curve settings
- normalisation to cell number or to protein

## Purpose

One reference for every surface coating used in the papers, so a new member picks the right one for the cell type and the assay.

## Coatings by cell type

| Cells and assay | Coating as stated in the paper |
|---|---|
| SH-SY5Y neurons, all assays | Poly-L-lysine (PLL) hydrobromide (Sigma P2636), 5 µg/cm² in ddH2O, 1 hour at room temperature. Remove and air-dry. Then Cultrex Basement Membrane Matrix (Bio-Techne 3434-010-02), 5 µg/cm² in ice-cold DMEM, 1 hour at 37 °C. Discard it immediately before seeding. |
| Chicken CGNs, culture and exposure | PLL, 3 µg/cm² in MQ water, 60 minutes (Sigma P2636 in Zosen 2022, Sigma P9155 in Labba 2022). Aspirate and dry before plating. |
| Chicken CGNs and NT2N, immunocytochemistry | PLL as above, then a second layer of Geltrex (ThermoFisher A1413302) "applied according to manufacturer's instructions". The PLL is not allowed to dry before the Geltrex goes on, and the Geltrex is not allowed to dry before seeding. |
| NT2N, MTT assay | PLL as above, then 320 ng/cm² fibronectin (ThermoFisher 33010018) applied in half of the intended seeding volume. Cells are seeded directly into the coating solution. |
| NT2N spheroid migration | PLL and fibronectin, as above (protocol 09) |
| CGN spheroid migration | PLL only |
| NTERA2 maintenance flasks | Gelatin (Sigma G1890-500G) in MQ water for 1 hour at room temperature, then removed and washed with PBS. The paper prints the density as "1.33 mg/cm2". Confirm before use. |
| PC12, 2016 | Collagen type IV (Sigma), for six-well plates and for slides placed in Petri dishes |
| PC12, 2018 | Rat-tail collagen (Roche 11 179 179 001). The concentration is not stated. |
| PC12, 2021, high-content assay | Collagen bio-coat BD Falcon 96-well plates |
| Rat hippocampal NSCs | Collagen type IV (Roche). The concentration is not stated. |
| Human iPSCs, 6-well plates | Cultrex ECM, 10 µg/cm² (protocol 06) |

## General procedure

1. Calculate the volume from the surface area of the vessel and the coating density.
2. Add the solution to every well and incubate for the stated time and temperature.
3. For PLL: remove the solution and air-dry the surface before cell seeding. For layered coatings with Geltrex or fibronectin, follow the order and the "do not dry" rules in the table.
4. Seed cells as soon as the final coating step ends.

## Not stated in the papers

- concentrations for the collagen coatings in the PC12 and NSC cultures
- the working dilution of Geltrex
- whether the dried plates were stored before use

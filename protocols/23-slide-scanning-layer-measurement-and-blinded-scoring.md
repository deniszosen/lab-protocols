## Purpose

Scan stained slides, measure the thickness of cerebellar layers, score PAX6 expression and count mitotic and apoptotic nuclei in a way that two independent observers can reproduce without knowing the treatment.

## Materials

- Pannoramic MIDI slide scanner (3DHistech) with a 20× objective, and 3DHistech Case Viewer.
- IBM SPSS Statistics 26 for the reliability analysis.

## Procedure

### Blinding

1. Label every sample without reference to its treatment group (A01, A02, A03 and so on). Give the observers minimal background information.
2. Two researchers evaluate the sections independently and blind.

### Scanning

3. Scan the slides (H&E and PAX6, protocols 20 and 21) with the 20× objective.

### Layer thickness

4. Measure the thickness of the external granular layer (EGL), molecular layer (ML), internal granular layer (IGL), the whole cerebellar cortex (EGL to IGL) and the deep white matter (WM), on the digital images in Case Viewer.
5. Measure perpendicular to the pial surface, as defined in the 2025 paper: EGL from the pial surface to the EGL/ML border, ML from the EGL/ML border to the ML/IGL border, IGL from the ML/IGL border to the white matter.
6. Measure at **five locations per tissue section** with optimal orientation, in **two sections per animal**.

### PAX6 scoring (2022)

7. In the IGL, count the PAX6-positive and PAX6-negative nuclei per 192 × 99 µm area and grade the staining intensity as negative, low, moderate or high.
8. Count migrating neurons in the molecular layer. Do the PAX6 evaluation and the molecular layer counts at three locations per section.

### Mitoses and apoptosis (2025)

9. Count mitotic nuclei in the EGL and apoptotic nuclei in the IGL on H&E sections.

### Reliability

10. Assess the interobserver variability with the intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC) in SPSS. Strong agreement is 0.8 to 1 and moderate is 0.5 to 0.8. If the agreement is moderate, assign a consensus score. Both papers report strong agreement for the measurements.

## Replicates

The 2022 study repeated the experiments in three independent setups with at least three animals (biological replicates) per injection group, so n was at least 9. Embryos with the highest and lowest body mass were excluded (protocol 10).

## Not stated in the papers

- the criteria to identify a mitotic or apoptotic nucleus
- how the measurement locations were chosen
- the ICC model and type used in SPSS
